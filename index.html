About Cheryl

Born on land.
Built for the water.

With twenty years of professional photography and a degree in Graphic Design from Melbourne University in Australia, I bring a trained eye for composition to every frame — whether it's a sunset proposal on the bow or a large group celebration on the main deck.

I own my own vessel, which doubles as my studio for extensive editing sessions. I'm constantly embedded with charter crews and yacht companies capturing weddings, private parties and pure joy on the water in San Diego, California. Available for charters, events, and destinations worldwide.
